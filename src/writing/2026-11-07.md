I have always had _some_ sort of art practice in my about-20 years of being \(mostly\) fully conscious. In my past half-decade of being an \"adult\" with bills and a job and responsibilities and relationships to tend to, my practices have been persistent but inconsistent.

Fortunately, I have a partner and a host of friends who have consistent practices and dedications, and over the past month I found my groove and joined the host of beautiful people who have a regular practice and can discuss the things I actively do and have things to show that I am, at least, somewhat proud to share. Life comes at you and affects you from an innumerable amount of directions at any given time, and I hate oversimplications, but I do enjoy a good simplificiation every now and then, and I think I\'ve found one to use as a lens into the perspective shift that has encouraged me to be more regular and rooted in my practices. It is _consumption_, specifically _being consumed_.

Some personal history: I grew up in a heavily Pentecostal family. This was mostly my extended family, my immediate family was pretty chill about it. That being said, I know what it feels like to catch the Holy Ghost and be so overcome with a fire that what you do is no longer what _you_ are doing. Fire shut up in your bones. As I\'ve been regulating my practice, I have been innately pulled to consider and remember feelings from adolecense, and the feeling of my doing my old practices has been indisguinshible from me being at the altar speaking in tongues. This could also be hazy memory, but also hindsight can be clarifying. Often this was at my detriment in both scenarios: drugs to stay awake for working on a design, visualizing Hell to invoke a fear of God in me so deep that I\'m spooked into the spirit, ignoring bodily queues during prayer or while trying to get an idea out. There is something extremely beautiful and useful about being able to get fully enraptured in an action or pursuit of a state-of-being though.

As a, fortunately, well-regulated grown man now, letting something become my world, or just trying to reach that state even if only for half an hour, has been doing wonders for me so far with minimal side effects. I\'m really not one for life-hacks or gimmicks, so I\'m trying not to over-index on it. I do enjoy a good simplification, however.

_How_ to become consumed is an entirely different question, and only your god can answer that for you.
