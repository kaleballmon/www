---
title: "You wouldn't believe where I've been"
date: 2026-11-19
date_color: "#461D7C"
---

I am a young black man from Baton Rouge, Louisiana and I have forged experiences
for myself that even I am overwhelmed by or can't fully internalize at times.

At my aunt's graduation last year I saw an old friend and he told me to give him
the rundown of what my life has been in the past 10 years and I couldn't
physically bring myself to do that.
