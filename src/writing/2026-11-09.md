If you\'ve heard me talk you know that I have a southern twang on several words and if you have gotten the chance to get me excited in a conversation you know that I will bust out the occasional stutter at the beggining of my sentence. A secret is that I have a great command over both, but I give them more space depending on the situation. Sometimes I allow my speech impediment out a bit more to test the waters of a person and sometimes I let the Louisiana really shine to let folks know where I\'m from and what my point of view is. Almost like putting a mood ring on someone and watching it change but only I have the sheet of paper and know how to read the colors.
