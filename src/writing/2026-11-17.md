I deleted all social media a year and a half ago, I no longer watch YouTube, or play online chess. I rarely drink anymore, and over the past 6 months
I\'ve almost forgotten how to roll a joint. And now basketball is taking up so much of my physical and mental capacity. I love it.
I feel transported back to 8th grade, rotating between an neverending roulette of NBA TV, pickup games at my grandpa\'s gym, basketball practice,
and NBA 2K sessions.

My hoop dreams ended early. I never played organized ball beyond 9th grade. At this point of my life, more than a decade after I hung it up,
basketball has never been _less_ serious for me, but it brings me the most enjoyment it has ever brought me.
One thing no one talks about when quitting your vices is that your soul still craves pure fun and entertainment. I find fun within my art practices
or in leisure times with friends, but that type of fun typically has multiple functions for me, i.e. I\'m working on
a project, or fostering relationships, or devloping a new skill. When I play and watch basketball now, my _only_ focus is fun.

I hate to sound like the basketball-can-save-the-world-spokesperson AND I can confidently say my re-obsession with the game has fostered a village of friends, peers, mentors, and strangers in my life that give me
the juice to keep going and make me keep working to not lose my touch. Maybe this was my real hoop dream all along.

I\'m watching Jazz v. Clippers on NBA League Pass as I write this.
