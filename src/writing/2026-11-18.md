title: "For my brother: I've seen it all before"
date: 2026-11-18

This is me starting a tradition of a tribute to my brother every year on his
birthday.

A casual but significant part of moving for me is finding a new regular
community of people to play basketball with in your new area. You get a tabula
rasa to show what type of player you are, create your record, and build your
on-court lore. Outside of joining an adults league or something, I think it\'s
the closest thing a non-professional basketball player can get to a personal new
basketball season.

In my several seasons within the Central Brooklyn pickup basketball association,
one thing that stays absolutely consistent is that I get First Team All-Defense
honors and that is because of my brother. My brother was a star high school
basketball player and also played all 4 years of college. He was the of player
who was on high-school varisty in 8th grade. For a while, he was easily the best
basketball player our family had seen; our little cousin is nipping at his heels
though. He is also 4 and a half years older than me and about 3 inches taller
and I don\'t even know how I would start to quantify the amount of times I\'ve
had to guard him or play him 1-1 since I was 10 years old. Because of that,
defense has always been a light task.
